### jju1s

I build small offline tools for people who live in AI chat apps.

Everything here is free and open source. No accounts, no servers, no telemetry,
nothing that phones home. If one of them saves you some time and you feel like
saying thanks, there is a Ko-fi link at the bottom. That is the whole business
model, and the tools stay free either way.

---

**[Carryover](https://github.com/Lrnssssss/carryover)** · Chrome extension

Shows how full your ChatGPT, DeepSeek, or Grok conversation is getting, then
compacts it into a handoff document you can paste into a new chat. It declares
no permissions in the manifest and makes no network requests, so it cannot leak
your conversations. The summary is pulled out of the text with pattern matching
rather than generated by a model, which means it cannot invent a decision you
never made and carry that fiction into your next chat.

[Download a build](https://github.com/Lrnssssss/carryover/releases) · MIT licensed
